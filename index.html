Mushroom Field Study, Solan

A field visit to Solan, Himachal Pradesh, to meet institutes and real mushroom growers and bring back a baseline that can be compared with Kurukshetra — especially Thanesar and Pehowa.

This site is part of Vipul Anand’s Kurukshetra / Haryana mushroom diversification study, which looks at stepping off the wheat–paddy cycle toward a wider mix of crops.

Travel window: after 16 November 2026 Suggested dates: 23–25 November 2026 Motorcycle from Kurukshetra

Read the field-trip plan Main study on GitHub

Purpose of the visit

Meet scientists and actual growers around Solan, see composting, spawn and crop rooms where a visit is allowed, and gather first-hand figures for comparison with the Haryana plains.

Planned window

The trip is after 16 November 2026, for one or two nights at most. The preferred working window is Monday 23 to Wednesday 25 November, after a women-only DMR course on 18–20 November.

What is here now

The field-trip plan has contacts, the motorcycle route, late-November weather, stay options, a packing list and a grower questionnaire. Phone numbers are copied from published pages and still need to be confirmed by phone. Nothing has been booked or arranged.

More study material is coming. Further notes and work will be added to this repository. This first published set is the landing page and the field-trip plan only.
