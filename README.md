# Mushroom Field Study, Solan

A field-visit annex to [Vipul Anand’s Kurukshetra / Haryana mushroom diversification study](https://github.com/anandvip/mushroom-haryana). The trip is meant to meet institutes and actual growers around Solan and bring back a baseline that can be compared with Thanesar and Pehowa.

Travel window: after 16 November 2026. Suggested dates: 23–25 November 2026.
